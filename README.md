## 🧭 What I work on

I am a **thesis-based Master of Computer Science student**, Graduate Research Assistant, and Teaching Assistant at [Concordia University](https://www.concordia.ca/), working with [Prof. Hovhannes Harutyunyan](https://users.encs.concordia.ca/~haruty/) in the **Algorithms & Complexity Lab** in Montréal, Canada.

My thesis, **_Geodesic-Compact Community Detection and Quality Assessment in Social Networks_**, is expected to be completed in 2027. My work sits at the intersection of **theoretical computer science**, **graph theory**, **combinatorial optimization**, and **computational social science**.

## 🧪 Research highlights

- **Spider:** Designed a community detection algorithm combining geodesic expansion, modularity-guided refinement, and greedy merge matching.
- **Benchmarking:** Evaluated Spider against Louvain, Leiden, Infomap, CNM, and Label Propagation on real-world networks and LFR benchmarks.
- **wGDM:** Introduced **Weighted Average Geodesic Distance Modularity**, a size-consistent, label-free partition-quality measure.
- **Metric backbones:** Achieved **14–71% edge reduction** across seven networks while preserving or improving detection quality.
- **Reproducibility:** Built an experimental pipeline with fixed seeds, baseline implementations, and automated evaluation scripts.
