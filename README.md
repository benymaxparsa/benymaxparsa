## About

I’m a thesis-based **M.Sc. student in Computer Science** at [Concordia University](https://www.concordia.ca/) and a member of the [Algorithms & Complexity Lab](https://users.encs.concordia.ca/~haruty/).

My thesis is **_Geodesic-Compact Community Detection and Quality Assessment in Social Networks_**. I’m interested in theoretical computer science, graph algorithms, community detection, graph mining, and computational social science.

Currently:

- Researching graph community detection with **Prof. Hovhannes Harutyunyan**
- Teaching algorithms, databases, computer architecture, and more
- Expected to graduate in **2027**

## Research

My recent work includes:

- **Spider** — a community detection algorithm using geodesic expansion, modularity-guided refinement, and greedy merging
- **wGDM** — a label-free measure for evaluating community quality
- **Metric backbone sparsification** — reducing graph size while preserving or improving community detection quality
- Reproducible experiments across real-world networks and LFR benchmarks
