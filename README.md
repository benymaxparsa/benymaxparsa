## 🧭 What I work on

I am a thesis-based **Master's student and Teaching Assistant in Computer Science** at [Concordia University](https://www.concordia.ca/), working with [Prof. Hovhannes Harutyunyan](https://users.encs.concordia.ca/~haruty/) in the **Algorithms & Complexity Lab**. My teaching portfolio spans **10 courses and 1,100+ hours** in the classroom.

My research lives at the intersection of **theoretical computer science**, **graph theory**, and **network science**. I build, benchmark, and publish algorithms that reveal meaningful communities in social, citation, and synthetic networks.

```text
 messy graph  ──►  scalable algorithm  ──►  meaningful communities  ──►  better understanding
```

## 🔬 Research map

| Area | Questions I explore |
| --- | --- |
| **Algorithms** | How can combinatorial, approximation, and randomized methods scale? |
| **Graph mining** | How do we detect communities, predict links, and classify nodes? |
| **Network science** | What makes a community meaningful in a complex network? |
| **Graph machine learning** | How can embeddings and GNNs learn from large-scale structure? |

> **Design algorithms. Mine graphs. Understand networks.**
